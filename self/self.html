self

present, and not

I am Saurabh. I like to talk about the self: the one that is present, and the one that is not.

I set aside the information and perceptions that reach me second-hand. What I accept is what has been experienced by my own understanding.

And I am wrong about that too. My understanding is also borrowed. It is only that some of the borrowing I know about, and some I don't.

This page is a beginning. The rest is still being written.
